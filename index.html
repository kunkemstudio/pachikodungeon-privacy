Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Pachiko Dungeon: Arcane Bounce ("the App") is an arcade roguelite mobile game developed and published by Kun Kem Studio (Developer ID: 8209266442810936589). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure, transparent gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The App itself does not require registration, accounts, or collection of your name, phone number, contacts, or financial details. To ensure 120 FPS performance and support free gameplay, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs that collect limited diagnostic and non-personally identifiable advertising data:

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): A resettable, unique device-level identifier used to serve contextual and interest-based advertisements.
  • Device Information: Device model, screen resolution, operating system version, and approximate location (country/city level derived from IP) for regional ad serving and language localization.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Impressions, views, and clicks to measure ad delivery performance.

Review Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Services & Crash Diagnostics

  • Crash Logs & Performance Data: Anonymous telemetry and crash stack traces used exclusively to diagnose ANRs (Application Not Responding) and ensure fluid 120 FPS game rendering.

2. How We Use Information

  • Deliver high-quality banner and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Monitor game stability and eliminate physics engine anomalies across Android devices.
  • Save high scores and game progress locally via Android Jetpack DataStore (stored strictly on-device).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

3. Data Encryption & Security

All network communications between the App, Google AdMob, and Google Play Services are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data is handled in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Pachiko Dungeon: Arcane Bounce is designed and rated for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly target or collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe personal information from a child has inadvertently been processed, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.

5. Your Rights & Advertising ID Management

You maintain full control over your advertising identifier. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time directly through your device settings:

  • Navigate to: Settings > Google > Ads
  • Tap: Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID
